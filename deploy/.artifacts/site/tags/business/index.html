Resume

Hiring is an anxiety-producing process. You do not know me, I do not know you, and a poor match costs us both. This page is meant to reduce that uncertainty by laying out, plainly, where my work has taken me and what I do well.

Positioning

I work at the intersection of three recurring emphases:

  • Building, debugging, and improving real systems across frontend, backend, infrastructure, and documentation Software Development
  • Translating technical ambiguity into plans, estimates, workflows, and decisions that non-technical stakeholders can use Business
  • Preparing, supporting, and strengthening teams with practical care, prayer, leadership, and follow-through Christian Mission
