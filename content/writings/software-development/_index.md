Software development is never only about code. It is about shaping systems that can survive contact with real users, shifting requirements, and the accumulated cost of unclear decisions.

These essays cover architecture, tooling, frontend and backend work, delivery habits, and the engineering patterns that make complex systems easier to build, debug, and sustain.
