Hiring is an anxiety-producing process. You do not know me, I do not know you, and a poor match costs us both. This page is meant to reduce that uncertainty by laying out, plainly, where my work has taken me and what I do well.

## Positioning

I work as a senior consultant at the intersection of three recurring emphases:

- Building, debugging, and improving real systems across frontend, backend, architecture, infrastructure, and documentation
- Translating ambiguity into clear requirements, visual models, plans, workflows, and decisions that leaders can use
- Strengthening leaders, teams, and communities through practical care, prayer, discipleship, facilitation, and follow-through

Across the years, those three domains have not been separate tracks. Software work sharpened my systems thinking. Business consulting taught me how to elicit needs, reduce confusion, and organize action. Christian mission forced those same instincts into the human realities of trust, formation, leadership, and courage. The throughline is disciplined clarity in service of meaningful change.
